## Domain guidance: Contract for Difference terms

Indian renewable-energy tenders, common ground for every section:

- Agencies name the same thing differently. SECI: RfS, Bid Information Sheet, SCSD or SCD, Effective Date, CfDA. Amounts are often written in the Indian system: 1 lakh = 100,000; 1 crore = 10,000,000. Give plain rupees and say in the rationale what the document printed.
- "As per NIT", "as per the portal" or "to be notified" is not a value. Return null and say which document will set it.
- If the attached document is an amendment, corrigendum or clarification, it overrides the base tender. Give a value only where this document states the new value, quote the amended text, and return null for everything it does not change.

A Contract for Difference (CfD) tender does not buy the power: the developer sells it on a power exchange and the procurer's CfD Pool settles the difference between the market clearing price (MCP) and the strike price (SP) the developer bid.

- `settlement_rule`: write how the gain (MCP above SP) and the loss (MCP below SP) are settled, as the pages state it: a full pass-through (the developer always nets the strike price), or sharing ratios by price slab; quote the clause.
- `strike_price_ceiling_inr_per_kwh`: the highest tariff a bid may quote. When the pages say the tariff "has to be less than or equal to" a figure, that figure is the ceiling; when no ceiling is stated, null.
- `daily_energy_kwh_per_mw`: the energy the developer must sell per MW per day ("2000 kWh of energy per MW Contracted Capacity" is 2000). `peak_hours_per_day` is how many hours the developer chooses within `peak_window`.
- `storage_per_mw` and `storage_mwh_per_mw`: the storage mandated per contracted MW ("X MW / 2X MWh" is 2 MWh per MW); say in the rationale whether the developer must own it.
- `markets_allowed`: every market segment named for the sale (DAM, GDAM, HP-DAM, RTM, TAM, G-TAM), one item each.
- `green_market_share_pct`: the share of annual energy that may come from the green market or bilateral agreements; further time-limited allowances go in the rationale.
- `re_commissioning_months` only when the generating components have a deadline of their own, later than the storage's SCD.
- `term_years`: the term of the CfD agreement from the SCD, as amended.
- `weekly_tolerance_pct` and `shortfall_penalty_rule`: the tolerance before a penalty and the penalty as written, with the reference price it uses.
