Privacy

What this site counts

This notice covers the public site tenderos.joulewise.tech. The TenderOS application itself runs on your own premises and sends jouleWise nothing.

Visits

Visits are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, a service of our hosting provider. It sets no cookies and keeps no advertising profile. For each page view it records the page, the site you came from, the country, and the kind of device and browser. Visitors are told apart by a hash that is reset every day, so a visit today cannot be joined to a visit tomorrow.

The site also records what was used: that the guided tour was started and which screens were reached, that the deck was downloaded, that the phone number or the demo button was pressed, which sections were read and roughly how long the page stayed open. Nothing you type is recorded: not the design studio's numbers, and not the demo request, which opens in your own mail application and is sent from there.

Personal links

If jouleWise sent you a link of the form tenderos.joulewise.tech/for/a-name, that name is a label for you or your company. It is kept in your browser for 90 days and attached to the events above, so that we know the person we wrote to has looked at the site and what interested them. We use it to follow up with you and for nothing else. A visit without such a link carries no label.

Your choice

These switches act on this browser only. To have what was recorded under your personal link deleted, or to ask anything else, write to sales@joulewise.com or call +91 87967 92724.
