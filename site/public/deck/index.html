The problem

Hundreds of pages, always changing

SECI CfD-I: an RfS of 129 pages, a 69-page agreement and four amendments in four months. One removed the ceiling tariff; one let SECI cut the award to its corpus.

Spreadsheets rebuilt every time

Each amendment sends the bid team back to its model. Nobody can say which page a number came from or which input moved the tariff.

Bids lost on the price

Awarded tariffs cluster within paise. An over-built battery or a high return target puts a bid out of the auction before it starts.

The battery becomes mandatory and yoursAmendment-02 · 4.3 · p.1

Storage is no longer optional or leasable: for every contracted MW you build and own at least 1 MW / 2 MWh of battery.

No ceiling tariffAmendment-03 · 40.3 · p.4

The ₹5.80/kWh ceiling and the summary rejection above it are removed; one first-round tariff, two decimals.

Settlement: full pass-throughAmendment-03 · 9.3.a.iv · p.2

The slab sharing (70:30, 50:50, 30:70) goes: the Pool takes the whole gain when the market clears above the strike price and pays the whole shortfall when it clears below, so you net the strike price on every kWh settled. Amendment-01's split above ₹10/kWh is replaced with it.
