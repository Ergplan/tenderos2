For the jouleWise sales team

Make a personal link

Give each prospect their own link. Their visits and what they used on the site are then recorded under that label, in Vercel Analytics, for as long as their browser keeps it (90 days from the last time they open the link).

The label is visible to the prospect in the address bar: use a company name or initials, nothing you would not show them. Open links you are only testing in a private window, or your own browser will carry the label.

Where to see who came

In this site's Vercel Analytics. Under Pages, a personal visit shows as /for/the-label. Under Events, open any event (Personal link, Tour screen, Deck downloaded, Call tapped) and break it down by ref.
